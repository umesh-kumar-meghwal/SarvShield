THREAT AWARENESS

Common Scam Vectors

Recognize the deceptive patterns used by modern financial fraudsters.

Payment & UPI Fraud

Fake QR codes, "Enter PIN to receive money", and spoofed transaction receipts.

Phishing & Fake Logins

Spoofed bank portals, electricity bill notices, and fake PAN/KYC update URLs.

Part-Time Job Scams

"Like YouTube videos & earn ₹5000/day" task traps demanding deposit payments.

Lottery & Customs Scams

KBC lottery messages, fake parcel clearance fees, and digital arrest threats.
