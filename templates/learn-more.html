DIGITAL SAFETY GUIDE

Learn how to spot deception.

Scams are designed to make you react before you think. Learn the warning signs, understand common scam techniques, and protect yourself from digital fraud.

01

Phishing

Phishing messages pretend to come from banks, companies or trusted people. They often contain suspicious links designed to steal your information.

Urgent requests Fake links Credential theft
02

Fake Authority

Scammers may pretend to be police officers, bank employees, government officials or customer support agents to create fear and pressure.

Impersonation Threats Pressure
03

Too Good To Be True

Fake rewards, lottery wins, investment opportunities and unbelievable discounts are common ways scammers attract victims.

Unexpected rewards Money requests Fake offers
04

Social Engineering

Instead of attacking a device, scammers often attack human emotions. Fear, urgency, curiosity and trust can be used to manipulate decisions.

Fear Urgency Manipulation

REMEMBER

The four rules of digital safety.

Pause.

Don't react immediately to urgent messages.

Verify.

Check who is actually contacting you.

Protect.

Never share OTPs, passwords or PINs.

Report.

Report suspicious activity instead of ignoring it.
